# Your 3 AI Bonuses
For members of Business Owner Workshops. Needs Claude Code and a paid Claude plan.

**Mac:** `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LaughlinBusinessAdvisors/lba-ai-bonuses/main/install.sh | bash`

**Windows (PowerShell):** `irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LaughlinBusinessAdvisors/lba-ai-bonuses/main/install.ps1 | iex`

It adds nine commands to Claude Code and makes a folder called My Business Brain. Your answers are saved on your computer and are processed by Claude under your own account. Leave out customer, employee and financial account information.
